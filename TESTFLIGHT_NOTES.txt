NEW: the Bulk and Ag pack, free in the beta. When you start a new career, choose Bulk earthworks: you start with a dozer or an excavator instead of a grader, and farmers and developers ring on the Work tab about dams, channels, contour banks, clearing, laser levelling, shed pads and bulk earthworks. Or start on Roads and add bulk work alongside (Career tab).

Also new: diesel. Put a bulk fuel tank in your yard (Yard tab) and buy loads cheaper than the pump, but keep it topped up: run dry and your machines sit idle waiting on the fuel truck. The price moves every week, and a war overseas can send it through the roof. An operations manager can keep the tank full for you.

And: DDES Advisory on every safety decision and to check tender prices; ProActive by DDES for regional depots; a Christmas shutdown; type your own margin; council suspensions now stop council work; free drivers cover for injured ones; clearer incident and buy-out cards; Run on handles the small stuff.

Machine rates now follow a real 2022-23 hire schedule on a 10-hour day, and the 627 scraper is in. Council gravel jobs now plan a loader at the pit: yours, dry hire or a subbie. Leave it off and the trucks wait their turn. Roadtrains: buy one, or put a second trailer behind a side tipper you own, or hire them in on council jobs. Some roads are singles only. With a fitter of your own, breakdowns get two more choices (they patch it, or fix it properly), and Run on makes that call for you by how good they are. Fixed: finished bulk jobs now clear off the Work screen, and the rain card talks wet dirt when bulk work is going.

NEW for launch: ads and the shop. The first time you open it, iPhone asks whether to allow tracking (either answer is fine). Google's TEST ads show in this build: a banner on the Market and Office tabs, and ads you choose to watch for a free mechanic on a breakdown, a late invoice paid now, or a weekly cash top-up (Office tab). Tap Ad choices in the Career tab to see Google's settings. The Bulk and Ag pack stays free in the beta.
