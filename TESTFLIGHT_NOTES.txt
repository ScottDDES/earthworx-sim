NEW: the Bulk and Ag pack, free in the beta. When you start a new career, choose Bulk earthworks: you start with a dozer or an excavator instead of a grader, and farmers and developers ring on the Work tab about dams, channels, contour banks, clearing, laser levelling, shed pads and bulk earthworks. Or start on Roads and add bulk work alongside (Career tab).

Also new: diesel. Put a bulk fuel tank in your yard (Yard tab) and buy loads cheaper than the pump, but keep it topped up: run dry and your machines sit idle waiting on the fuel truck. The price moves every week, and a war overseas can send it through the roof. An operations manager can keep the tank full for you.
