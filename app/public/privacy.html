Earthworx Sim privacy

Updated 21 October 2026

The short version

Earthworx Sim doesn't ask who you are. There's no sign-up, and we never sell anything about you. Your games are saved on your own device. The game on phones shows ads from Google, and Google collects some information to show them (below). The only thing the game itself sends anywhere is a season score you choose to post to the public board.

What stays on your device

  • Your careers, your season in progress and your settings (like sound).
  • A random player number the game makes the first time you open it, so the board can keep your best score. It isn't linked to your name, phone, email or Apple or Google account.
  • The nickname you last posted under, so you don't have to type it again.

Deleting the app (or clearing the website's data in your browser) deletes all of this.

What goes to the board, and only if you post

When you finish a season and tap Post my score, the game sends:

  • the nickname you typed and the company name you played under; both are shown publicly on the board, so don't use your real name unless you want to,
  • the moves you made that season, so the board can play them again and work out the score itself (the moves are used for that check and then thrown away; only how many there were is kept),
  • your random player number, which is stored only in scrambled form and never shown.

The board keeps your best score for each season, with the nickname, company name, score, weeks played and the date.

To stop spam, the board also notes a scrambled copy of your internet address each time you post. It's used only to limit how many scores can be posted in an hour, and it's deleted within a day.

Ads

The phone game shows a small banner on the Market and Office screens, and offers short ads you can choose to watch for something in the game (a free mechanic, a late invoice paid, a weekly cash top-up). The ads come from Google AdMob.

  • To show and measure ads, Google collects information from your phone, such as its advertising identifier, rough location from your internet address, and how you interact with the ads. Google's own policy covers what it does with it: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.
  • On an iPhone, Apple asks whether you allow tracking. Say no and you still get ads, just less suited to you. You can change it any time in Settings, Privacy and Security, Tracking.
  • Where the law asks for it (for example in Europe and the UK), Google asks for your consent the first time you open the game. You can change your answer under Ad choices in the Career tab.
  • Your game, your company name and your scores are never given to Google or anyone else for ads.

Buying No ads takes the banner away. The ads you choose to watch for a reward stay on offer.

Purchases

The Bulk and Ag pack and No ads are bought through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google handles the payment; we never see your card or account details. The game only learns which things you own, so it can unlock them on your phone.

Who runs it

Earthworx Sim is made by Darling Downs Earthmoving Solutions Pty Ltd (DDES) in Queensland, Australia. The board and the web version run on Cloudflare, which keeps the data and may keep short-lived technical logs to run its service. We don't share board data with anyone else, and we don't use it for anything but the board.

Kids

The game is fine for all ages and the game itself collects nothing that identifies anyone. It does show Google ads, so if you're young, ask a parent before saying yes to tracking or personalised ads, and pick a nickname that isn't your real name.

Taking a score down

Names on the board are checked for bad language, and we can hide any score. To have your score or nickname taken off the board, or to ask anything about your privacy, email scott@ddes.biz with the season and the nickname. We'll answer within 30 days. You can also complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).

Changes

If the game starts keeping anything new, this page will say so before it does, and the date at the top will change.
