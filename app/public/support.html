Get in touch

Something not working, a number that looks wrong, or an idea? Email scott@ddes.biz with your phone model and what happened. A screenshot helps. We answer within a few working days.

Purchases

  • Bought something on another phone, or reinstalled? Open the Career tab and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple or Google: reportaproblem.apple.com for the App Store, or your Google Play order history.

Ads

The banner shows on the Market and Office tabs only. No ads (Career tab) takes it away. Ads that give you something in the game, like a free mechanic, are always your choice. To change Google's ad settings, tap Ad choices in the Career tab. On an iPhone, tracking is under Settings, Privacy and Security, Tracking.

Your saves

Your careers are saved on your phone, in three save slots (Career tab). Deleting the app deletes them, so finish or note anything you care about first. Updates keep your saves.
